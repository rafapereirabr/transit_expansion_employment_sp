## Objective

Send the internal team by the morning of Tuesday, 1 September, a self-contained progress
presentation for review before advance circulation by Daniel
to the broader CAF team, supporting a 15–20 minute São Paulo report and a substantive discussion on
11 September.

## Interpretation of the messages

- This is a progress report for the expanded CAF meeting, not the one-page flagship box and not a
  replacement for the formal CAF report.
- Santiago is handled by a separate team using a fundamentally different design: municipality-level
  individual geography, a pre-2010 two-period DiD, and already-produced preliminary estimates.
  Parallel headings are useful, but numerical or methodological comparability is not a requirement.
- The broader CAF audience needs material in advance and should not be expected to remember the
  workplan. The presentation must therefore restate the research design, data, and methods before
  showing progress.
- The two agenda descriptions are compatible for production purposes: prepare for 20 minutes of
  presentation and 10–15 minutes of questions. The later message's “15–20 minutes” is a range, not
  a request to shorten the content below the 20-minute slot.
- “Slides or any document” leaves format open, but a short slide deck is the natural choice for a
  timed oral report and can also be reviewed asynchronously.
- The working deadline is the morning of 1 September for internal review. This is exactly ten days
  before the 11 September meeting and is more operational than the relative email language.
